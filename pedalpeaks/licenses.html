Audio recordings

The following gameplay loops were trimmed, filtered, converted to mono, and given circular crossfades from recordings provided by Mixkit:

  • Bike wheel spinning — Mixkit item 1613; used for bike_freehub_loop.wav.
  • Bike pedalling on street loop — Mixkit item 1603; used for bike_drivetrain_loop.wav.
  • Bike pedalling on the road — Mixkit item 1605; used for bike_road_loop.wav.

Used under the Mixkit Free License. Attribution is not required by that licence, but the sources are credited here with thanks. View the source collection.
