1. Who is responsible?

Keep a Tally is published by Christian Netthöfel, Dortmund, Germany (“we”, “us”); full details are in the Imprint. For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@netthoefel.it.

2. Information kept on your device

Your progress, best results per level, which districts you have cleared, a resumable level, and your achievements and your sound preferences are stored locally on your device in the app’s settings storage (UserDefaults). The game also keeps a locally cached copy of whether you have bought the full game, so it opens correctly without a connection.

This information is not sent to us or to any server we operate. Deleting the app removes it, subject to iOS backup behaviour.

3. iCloud sync

If you use iCloud on your device, the app syncs progress and achievements between your devices through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. Apple stores this data under your Apple ID; we do not receive it or have access to it. You can turn this off in iOS Settings under Apple ID → iCloud for the app. See Apple’s iCloud privacy information.

4. Purchases

The opening districts are free. The remaining districts are unlocked by a single one-time in-app purchase. Apple processes the payment and does not give us your payment-card details.

To offer, verify and restore that purchase, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier generated for your installation, your purchase and entitlement status, product identifiers, and limited technical information such as app version, device model, operating system version and IP address. It does not receive your name or email address from us.

This processing is necessary to deliver what you bought and to restore it on request (legal basis in the EU: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The app does not use this data for advertising or for tracking. See Apple’s App Store privacy information and the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing, hosting and retention

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data is shared only with Apple (App Store payments, iCloud) and RevenueCat (purchase and entitlement management), and with authorities or advisers where required by law.

Purchase records may be kept by Apple and RevenueCat as long as needed to restore purchases and meet legal obligations. Service providers may process data in countries other than yours, using safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

This public page is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit it on the web, GitHub may process standard connection and server-log information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Using the app does not require visiting this page.

6. Children

Keep a Tally is not designed to collect personal information from children. It has no ads, chat, social features or profile creation. Parents and guardians can manage purchase permissions through Apple’s Screen Time and Family Sharing controls.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to receive a portable copy, and to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority (for us, the LDI NRW in Düsseldorf, Germany). Because we hold no personal data ourselves, most requests concern data held by Apple or RevenueCat; we are happy to help you route them. Contact us at the address below.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised, and material changes will be communicated where required.
