How it plays

Every floor is a plan to be solved.

Between you and the shaft bottom are dips you cannot cross, tubs you can only push, and turnplates that swing a quarter turn when you put your shoulder to an arm. Later levels send a whole crew underground.

  • Push

    Tubs fill dips in the floor and make a road across the water, if you push them in the right order.

  • Turn

    Turnplates pivot a quarter turn from the side. Swing one the wrong way and you may have trapped yourself.

  • Par

    Every level has a par, and it is not a guess: it is the length of the shortest solution, proven by exhaustive search.
